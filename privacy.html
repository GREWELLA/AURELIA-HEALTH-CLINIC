PRIVACY & DPDP NOTICE

How we handle
your information.

Last updated: 10 October 2026 · Applies to this website and its appointment request form

AURELIA Health Clinic ("AURELIA", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal data responsibly, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). This notice explains what we collect through this website, why, how it's used, and the rights you have over it.

This page is a plain-language notice, not a substitute for independent legal advice. AURELIA should have this reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on it for full regulatory compliance.

1. What we collect

When you use the appointment request form on this site, we collect the information you choose to provide through the custom appointment form. The form sends it to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stores it in a Google Sheet controlled by the site operator. Information may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your preferred department, doctor, date and time
  • Any details you share about your reason for visit — this may include health-related information, which the DPDP Act treats with particular care

We only collect what you choose to type into the form. We do not currently use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this website.

2. Why we collect it

We use this information solely to:

  • Understand and respond to your appointment request
  • Contact you by phone, email or WhatsApp to confirm or reschedule your visit
  • Maintain a record of your enquiry for our front-desk and clinical scheduling

We do not use your information for marketing without separately asking you, and we do not sell your personal data to anyone.

3. Our legal basis: your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your free, specific, informed and unambiguous consent, given when you tick the consent checkbox and submit the appointment form. You may decline to provide any information you're not comfortable sharing — this may simply mean we have less context ahead of your visit.

4. Who else sees it

The custom appointment form sends submissions to a Google Apps Script web app and stores them in a Google Sheet configured by the site operator. Google may process or store this data as part of those services under its privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. Access to the spreadsheet should be restricted to authorised staff. We do not intend to share your information with other third parties unless required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We retain appointment request data for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for — generally, for the duration of your active care relationship with AURELIA, plus any period required by applicable medical record-keeping or tax laws. You can ask us to delete your data sooner; see Section 7.

6. Children's data

Our paediatric and adolescent care services mean we sometimes process a child's data. Where a request relates to a child (anyone under 18), submission of this form should be made by, or with the verifiable consent of, a parent or lawful guardian, in line with the DPDP Act's requirements for processing children's personal data.

7. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data once it's no longer needed for the purpose it was collected
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us directly, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer below. Withdrawing consent won't affect the lawfulness of anything we did with your data before the withdrawal.

9. Security

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our practices evolve or as required by law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.
